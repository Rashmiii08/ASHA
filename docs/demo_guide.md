# Demo guide

1. Open the app, choose English or Hindi, then start a voice visit.
2. For the synthetic transcript demo, choose **Use sample** and **Send transcript**. Show the AI disclosure, structured fields, and fixed referral warning.
3. Send a correction such as `No, temperature is 36.9.` and show the updated draft.
4. Confirm and save. Open Visit history and Follow-ups, then mark the follow-up complete.
5. In a second draft, disconnect the network, reload the cached screen, restore connectivity and restart the session to resume. With the page left open, the socket retries automatically.

For a 2–3 minute recording, spend 20 seconds on the ASHA documentation workflow, 90 seconds on the visit and correction, and 30 seconds on history, follow-up and offline recovery. Label synthetic mode on screen. If live Gnani is available, replace typed transcript injection with a spoken synthetic note, read-back and interrupt, and show measured latency. Use a maximum of six slides covering the problem, field workflow, architecture, safety and data handling, demo and measured results, and remaining validation.
